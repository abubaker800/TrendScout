# Chrome Web Store listing

**Title:** TrendScout – Product Research & Store Lister
**Short description (≤132 chars):** Find trending products by country, estimate sales, ads and margins, and list to your store only with your approval.

**Long description:**
TrendScout helps ecommerce sellers find products worth selling — and keeps you in control of your store.

🔥 Trending Products Finder — browse trending products by country and category with trend score, estimated monthly sales, ad status and source marketplace. Search, filter (price, score, ads, seasonality), sort and export to CSV.
📈 Sales Estimator — low / expected / high monthly units from review velocity and store traffic, with a confidence rating and editable assumptions.
📣 Ad Spy — see whether a product is advertised on Facebook/Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest, with ad counts, first-seen dates and one-click links to the public ad libraries. Products with ads on 2+ platforms are flagged Hot.
💰 Profit Calculator — fees, shipping, ad cost and refunds → profit per unit, net margin, ROI, break-even ROAS and monthly profit, colour-coded.
🗓 Seasonality — 12-month and 5-year charts, automatic Year-round / Seasonal / Fad classification and a best-time-to-launch suggestion.
🔐 Owner-approved listing — connect Shopify or WooCommerce with minimum permissions. Every listing needs your explicit confirmation, is created as a draft by default, and is recorded in an audit log. Revoke access and delete all data with one click.
★ Watchlist, dark mode, side panel and an optional page-scanner overlay for Amazon, AliExpress and Shopify product pages.

Works instantly with demo data; add your own API keys for live data. Estimates are approximations, not guarantees. TrendScout is not affiliated with Amazon, AliExpress, Shopify, Meta, TikTok, Pinterest or Google.
